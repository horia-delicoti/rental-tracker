# Fiscal years

The fiscal year runs **April to March**, and the month of a line's date decides which year it
belongs to:

| Month of the date | Fiscal year |
| --- | --- |
| April 2025 – December 2025 | 2025/26 |
| January 2026 – March 2026 | 2025/26 |
| April 2026 | 2026/27 |

January, February and March belong to the year that started the *previous* April. This is the one
rule that is easy to get wrong by hand and the reason the app exists in this shape: a March payment
filed under the wrong year moves money between two tax returns.

Years appear as tabs across the top. A year gets a tab if it has any income or any expenses, plus
the current one, plus any future year added with **+ Year** — useful for recording a payment that
arrives early.

## What is computed per year

- **Income, expenses, profit** — combined, and per property
- **Owner share and tax** — see [Tax split](tax.md)
- **Spent** — expenses as a percentage of income
- **Deltas** — each figure against the same figure last year, when last year had any activity
- **Run rate** — for the current year only, what the year lands at if the remaining months look
  like the months already recorded

**Lifetime** totals at the bottom of the Overview are the sum of every year, each counted once.
